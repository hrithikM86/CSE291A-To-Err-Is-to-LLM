Requirements:
- Function: `rounded_avg(n: int, m: int) -> str or int`
- Inputs: Two positive integers `n` and `m`.
- Output: A binary string representing the rounded average of integers from `n` to `m` inclusive, or `-1` if `n > m`.
- Behavior:
  - Calculate the average of integers from `n` to `m`.
  - Round the average to the nearest integer.
  - Convert the rounded average to a binary string prefixed with "0b".
  - If `n > m`, return `-1`.
- Edge Cases:
  - `n` and `m` are positive integers.
  - `n` can be greater than, equal to, or less than `m`.
  - Handle the case where the average is exactly halfway between two integers.
