Requirements:
- **Input**: A single integer `n` (1 < n, n is not a prime).
- **Output**: The largest prime factor of `n`.
- **Behavior**:
  - Factorize `n` to find its prime factors.
  - Return the largest prime factor.
- **Edge Cases**:
  - `n` is a power of a prime number (e.g., 2048 = 2^11).
  - `n` is a product of multiple primes (e.g., 13195 = 5 * 7 * 13 * 29).
  - `n` is a large number, ensuring the algorithm is efficient.
