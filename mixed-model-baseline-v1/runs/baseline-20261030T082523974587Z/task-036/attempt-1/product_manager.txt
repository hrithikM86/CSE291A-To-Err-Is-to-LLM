Requirements:
- **Input**: A single integer `n` (1 ≤ n ≤ 10000).
- **Output**: An integer representing the number of times the digit 7 appears in integers less than `n` that are divisible by 11 or 13.
- **Behavior**:
  - Count the occurrences of the digit 7 in numbers less than `n` and divisible by 11 or 13.
  - Return the total count.
- **Edge Cases**:
  - If `n` is less than 1 or greater than 10000, the function should handle these cases appropriately (e.g., by raising an exception or returning a specific value).
