Requirements:
- **Inputs**: Three positive integers representing the lengths of the sides of a potential triangle.
- **Output**: A float rounded to 2 decimal points representing the area of the triangle, or -1 if the sides do not form a valid triangle.
- **Behavior**:
  - Calculate the semi-perimeter and use Heron's formula to find the area if the sides form a valid triangle.
  - Return -1 if the sides do not satisfy the triangle inequality theorem.
- **Edge Cases**:
  - All sides are zero or negative: Return -1.
  - Sides do not satisfy the triangle inequality theorem: Return -1.
  - Valid triangle: Return the area rounded to 2 decimal points.
