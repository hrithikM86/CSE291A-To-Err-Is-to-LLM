Requirements:
- **Input**: A non-empty list of integers (`lst`).
- **Output**: An integer representing the sum of all odd elements that are located at even indices in the list.
- **Behavior**:
  - Iterate through the list, considering only elements at even indices (0, 2, 4, ...).
  - Sum up the values of these elements if they are odd.
- **Edge Cases**:
  - If there are no odd elements at even indices, return 0.
  - The list will always contain at least one element.
