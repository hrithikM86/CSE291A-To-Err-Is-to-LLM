Algorithm for `simplify(x, n)`:

1. Parse `x` and `n` to extract numerators and denominators.
2. Calculate the product of the numerators and denominators separately.
3. Check if the product of the numerators is divisible by the product of the denominators.
4. Return `True` if the product of the numerators is divisible by the product of the denominators, otherwise return `False`.

Edge cases:
- `1/1` and `2/2` should return `True`.
- Empty strings or invalid formats should raise an error.
- Denominators cannot be zero.

Complexity:
- Parsing the fractions is O(1) since the format is fixed.
- Division and modulus operations are O(1).
- Overall complexity is O(1).

Ensure the function handles all edge cases as specified.
