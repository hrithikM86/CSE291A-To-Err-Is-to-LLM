Requirements:
- Function: `right_angle_triangle(a, b, c)`
- Inputs: Three positive integers representing the lengths of the sides of a triangle.
- Output: A boolean value (`True` or `False`).
- Behavior: 
  - Return `True` if the sides form a right-angled triangle.
  - Return `False` otherwise.
- Edge Cases:
  - All sides are positive integers.
  - Sides do not form a valid triangle (e.g., one side is greater than or equal to the sum of the other two).
  - Any side length is zero or negative.
