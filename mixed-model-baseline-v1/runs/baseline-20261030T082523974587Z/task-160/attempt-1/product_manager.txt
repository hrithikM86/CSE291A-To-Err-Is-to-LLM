Requirements:
- The function `do_algebra` takes two inputs: `operator` (a list of strings representing algebraic operations) and `operand` (a list of non-negative integers).
- The length of `operator` is one less than the length of `operand`.
- The function evaluates the algebraic expression formed by `operator` and `operand` and returns the result.
- Supported operations: `+` (addition), `-` (subtraction), `*` (multiplication), `//` (floor division), `**` (exponentiation).
- Edge cases:
  - Empty `operator` or `operand` lists should not occur due to the note constraints.
  - Non-integer values in `operand` should not occur as they are non-negative integers.
  - Invalid operators in `operator` should result in undefined behavior, but are not expected based on the given constraints.
