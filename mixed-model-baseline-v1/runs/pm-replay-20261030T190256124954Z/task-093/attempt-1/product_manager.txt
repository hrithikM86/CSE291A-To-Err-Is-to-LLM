Requirements:
- **Input**: A string `message` consisting of letters only.
- **Output**: A string representing the encoded message.
- **Behavior**:
  - Swap the case of all letters in the message.
  - Replace each vowel (a, e, i, o, u) with the letter that is 2 places ahead in the alphabet.
- **Edge Cases**:
  - If a vowel is at the end of the alphabet ('u' or 'U'), wrap around to the beginning ('a' or 'A').
  - Ignore non-letter characters.
  - Maintain the original spacing and punctuation.
