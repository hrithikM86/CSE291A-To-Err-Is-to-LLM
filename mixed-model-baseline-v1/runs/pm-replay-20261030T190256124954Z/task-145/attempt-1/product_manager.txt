Requirements:
- **Inputs**: A list of integers (`nums`).
- **Outputs**: A list of integers sorted based on the ascending order of the sum of their digits. If two numbers have the same digit sum, maintain their original order.
- **Behavior**:
  - Sort the list by the sum of digits of each number.
  - If two numbers have the same digit sum, keep their original order.
- **Edge Cases**:
  - An empty list should return an empty list.
  - Negative numbers should have their digits summed correctly, including negative signs.
  - Single-digit numbers should be handled as their digit sum is the number itself.
